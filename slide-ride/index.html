FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Durex Slide & Ride Sleeve?

A soft, flexible manual sleeve for men, with an internal dotted and ribbed texture.

What is it made from?

Thermoplastic elastomer (TPE). It is designed to be soft and stretchy.

How do I use it?

Wash before use, apply a water-based lubricant to the opening and inside, and gently squeeze out excess air. Control the movement by hand. Follow the instructions on the pack.

Does it vibrate or need charging?

No. Slide & Ride is a manual sleeve. It has no motor, charging cable or vibration controls.

Is Slide & Ride waterproof?

Yes. The sleeve is 100% waterproof and can be rinsed with water.

Which lubricant should I use?

Choose a water-based lubricant. Avoid oil-based or silicone-based lubricants, massage oils and hand creams.

How do I clean and store it?

Wash with warm water and mild soap, then dry with a lint-free cloth. Store dry in a cool place, protected from dust, direct sunlight and heat.

What comes in the pack?

One Slide & Ride sleeve and a discreet travel pouch.

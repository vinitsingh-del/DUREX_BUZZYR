FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Ride & Vibe made of? Is it safe for my skin?

It uses smooth, body-safe silicone and a textured interior.

How do I use the Ride & Vibe?

Hold the buzzing or pulsating button for 3 seconds to start. Tap to change settings. Both functions can operate together.

How long does Ride & Vibe last on a full charge?

Up to 60 minutes on a full charge.

How do I charge Ride & Vibe?

Connect the supplied magnetic USB cable to a USB power source. The LED flashes during charging and stops when charging is complete.

Is Ride & Vibe waterproof?

Yes. Durex describes Ride & Vibe as 100% waterproof. Follow the care instructions on the packaging.

What makes Ride & Vibe different?

It combines buzzing and pulsating functions, adjustable speeds, a ribbed interior and a dedicated MAX-intensity button.

How do I turn Ride & Vibe on and off?

Hold either function button for 3 seconds to turn on. Hold MAX for 3 seconds to turn off.

Is it noisy?

Durex describes it as quiet, with a low hum.

How do I clean Ride & Vibe?

Use warm water and mild soap before and after use. Dry with a lint-free cloth, and store only when completely dry.
